Getting started
How do I create an account on Nexus Exchange?

To create an account, go to www.nexus-exchange.com and click “Sign Up.”
Fill in your personal details, set a secure password, and verify your email address. Once confirmed, your account will be activated.

How do I get verified?

Log in to your account and visit the "Verify Now" section.
Upload a valid government-issued ID and a selfie, then follow the on-screen instructions. Verification usually takes a few hours.

How do I start trading on Nexus Exchange?

Head to the “Trade”,“Futures” or “Markets” section.
Choose your trading pair (e.g., BTC/USDT), analyze the chart, and place an order using market or limit options. Track your trades on the dashboard.

2.How do I receive and send crypto on Nexus?

. To deposit: Go to Wallet > Receive, copy your wallet address or scan the QR code.

. To Withdrawal: Go to Wallet > Send, input the recipient’s wallet address, amount, and confirm. Double-check the network before sending.
